# plug-n-rag

**Claude knows your notes and reads only what it really needs for your question.**

plug-n-rag makes your Obsidian notes (and, if you like, other folders of documents) searchable for
Claude Code. Claude finds matching passages even when you use different words than the note, reads
only those sections and names the source with lines. That saves tokens. Your notes stay unchanged,
and nothing goes to additional services without your consent. Works in English and German; the
search model was chosen for its especially good German and handles English just as well.

**Install:** `/plugin install plug-n-rag --marketplace sign-u-p/plug-n-rag` (older Claude Code:
`/plugin marketplace add sign-u-p/plug-n-rag`, then `/plugin install plug-n-rag@plug-n-rag`).

**Get started:** in your vault's folder, type `/plug-n-rag:setup`. Claude walks you through it.

Full guide (installation, privacy, how it works, cloud sessions, limits):
<https://github.com/sign-u-p/plug-n-rag#readme>
